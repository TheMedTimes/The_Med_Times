Privacy Policy
Effective date: April 2026  ·  Last updated: April 2026
This policy explains what data TheMedTimes collects, why, and how it is used and protected.
1 Who We Are

TheMedTimes is a daily crossword puzzle application built for MBBS students in India. It is operated as an independent student project. References to "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to TheMedTimes.

The app is accessible at themedtimes.github.io/The_Med_Times and as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on mobile devices.

2 What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to make the app work. Specifically:

  • Google Account information — your name and email address, obtained when you sign in with Google. We use your name to display on the leaderboard.
  • College abbreviation — entered by you during onboarding, used to identify you on the leaderboard.
  • Year of study — selected by you during onboarding, used to tailor your experience.
  • Puzzle completion data — the date you completed a puzzle, the time taken, and whether you used the Reveal feature.
  • Leaderboard visibility preference — your choice of whether your name and college appear publicly.

We do not collect your phone number, location, device identifiers, or any sensitive personal information. We do not collect data from users under the age of 18.

3 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate TheMedTimes. Specifically:

  • Your name and college are shown on the leaderboard if you have opted in.
  • Your completion data is used to calculate your position on the daily leaderboard.
  • Your year of study is used to route you to the correct puzzle content.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the app. We do not sell your data to any third party.

4 Third-Party Services

TheMedTimes uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication backend. Your profile and completion data are stored on Supabase servers located in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Supabase is SOC 2 compliant. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google OAuth (via Supabase) — used for sign-in. Google processes your sign-in request and shares your name and email with us. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic request metadata. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
5 Cookies and Local Storage

TheMedTimes does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies.

We use the following browser storage:

  • Supabase session cookie — stores your login session so you stay signed in between visits. This is essential for the app to function.
  • localStorage (tmt_progress) — stores your in-progress puzzle state locally on your device only, so your work is not lost if you close the app mid-puzzle. This data never leaves your device.
6 Data Retention

Your profile and completion data are retained for as long as your account exists. Completion records are kept to maintain leaderboard history and allow streak calculation.

If you delete your account (see section 7), all your personal data is permanently deleted from our database within 30 days.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct your name or college — you can do this directly in the app via Settings.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us (see section 8).
  • Opt out of appearing on the leaderboard at any time via Settings → Account → Leaderboard visibility.

These rights are provided in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023.

8 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, want to request deletion of your data, or want to report a concern, please reach out:

📧 Email: themedicalpuzzler@gmail.com

We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 7 days.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app grows. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
