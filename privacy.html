Privacy Policy
Effective date: April 2026 · Last updated: April 2026
This policy explains what data TheMedTimes collects, why, and how it is used and protected.
1 Who We Are

TheMedTimes is a daily crossword puzzle application built for MBBS students in India, operated as an independent student project. The app is accessible at themedtimes.github.io/The_Med_Times and as a Progressive Web App on mobile devices.

2 What Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to make the app work:

  • Google Account information — your name and email, obtained when you sign in with Google.
  • College abbreviation — entered by you during onboarding, used for the leaderboard.
  • Year of study — selected by you during onboarding, used to tailor your experience.
  • Puzzle completion data — the date you completed a puzzle, time taken, and whether you used Reveal.
  • Leaderboard visibility preference — your choice of whether your name and college appear publicly.

We do not collect your phone number, location, device identifiers, or any sensitive personal information. We do not collect data from users under the age of 18.

3 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate TheMedTimes — showing your name on the leaderboard if opted in, calculating your position, and routing you to the correct puzzle content. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose. We do not sell your data.

4 Third-Party Services
  • Supabase — database and authentication backend, servers in Singapore (ap-southeast-1), SOC 2 compliant.
  • Google OAuth — used for sign-in. Google shares your name and email with us upon login.
  • GitHub Pages — app hosting. GitHub may log basic request metadata.
  • Sentry — error tracking. Anonymous error reports are sent to help us fix bugs. No personal data is included in error reports.
5 Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use:

  • Supabase session cookie — keeps you signed in between visits. Essential for the app to function.
  • localStorage (tmt_progress) — stores your in-progress puzzle state on your device only. Never leaves your device.
6 Data Retention

Your profile and completion data are retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently deleted from our database within 30 days.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. You can correct your name or college directly in Settings. To delete your account and all associated data, use the "Delete my account" option in Settings, or contact us. These rights are provided in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023.

8 Contact Us

Questions about this policy or data deletion requests:

We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 7 days.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app grows. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.
